Why this exists

Brand and CX marketers often hear the truth first in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali or Hinglish — on WhatsApp voice notes, Play Store reviews and support tickets — not in English dashboards. This tool is a listening workflow: capture → Sarvam transcribe/translate → theme & sentiment tags → CSV for the weekly CX ritual.

Built as a portfolio piece for a marketer who cares about customer listening (including shop-floor and service feedback loops). It does not invent case-study results; connect your own research.

1. Sarvam API key (optional for text demo)

Key is sent only to api.sarvam.ai. Voice upload uses Saaras speech-to-text with mode=translate (REST, short clips). Text uses Mayura /translate into English. Sarvam docs.

About Wispr Flow

You can draft CX summaries by speaking them into docs with Wispr Flow. Wispr’s developer API is not publicly open without enterprise approval, so transcription of customer audio here uses Sarvam Saaras instead. That split is intentional: Wispr for the marketer’s own drafting; Sarvam for Indian-language customer audio and text.
